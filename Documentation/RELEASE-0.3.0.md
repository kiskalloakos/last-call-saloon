# Last Call 0.3.0 — Bluff, Break & Escape

A major saloon update for **Windows x64** and **macOS Intel + Apple Silicon**.
Use **0.3.0 on every computer** in a match. Network protocol state-36 is incompatible with older builds.

## Bluff and betray

- The West room alternates **Coin Flip and spoken Bluff Dice**, announced before players choose rooms.
- Bluff Dice gives the speaker three secret dice and a private claim. The listener chooses **Believe** or **Don't Believe**; the result awards the combat opening. Human players bluff through voice chat.
- Speakers start their own throw. Animated cups and tumbling dice lead into the claim, judgment and reveal. Hidden rolls stay private until the reveal.
- Deputies are hidden, and the independent slot secretly rolls **Renegade or Swindler**. The Swindler earns a personal victory by convincing the Law to kill them. Only Outlaws know their teammates.

## Steal and escape

- The standalone safe becomes a **cash drawer at the Bar**. Every role can inspect it; Outlaws can conceal theft behind the same visible two-second check.
- Taking the money requires **three shared steps in three separate rounds**. Teammates can share the work, with at most one successful step per round.
- An escape is announced during discussion, leaving at least ten seconds to reconsider room plans. One headshot stops it; the carrier keeps the money for another attempt.
- The escaping player keeps a normal action to **shoot, heal, reload or pass**, including after every defender misses.
- Once all Bar actions finish and the escape cannot be stopped, the game cuts straight to the **Outlaw victory screen**. Timed healing finishes first; other rooms do not delay a guaranteed escape.
- Sheriff death wins for Outlaws unless the Renegade is the sole survivor. The posse and automatic round-limit ending are removed: matches continue until a normal victory condition is reached.

## Break the saloon

- **Window panes and individual backbar bottles shatter when shot**, with flying glass and positional breaking sounds.
- Shots can pass through shattered panes. Broken scenery stays synchronized across players, later rounds and reconnects, and repairs for a new match.
- Enclosed minigame rooms have solid walls, larger wall art and muffled cross-room sound. Exterior windows look onto the sunset.
- The Bar darkens gradually toward night over rounds 1–20 while keeping its lamps lit. The private smoking deck and other rooms keep their own atmosphere.

## Recover and regroup

- Occasional medical opportunities make room choices matter. A featured side room offers treatment through its normal minigame/combat rules.
- **Toilet Trouble** is an optional, isolated solo mission offered to injured players. Finish the plunger task to recover 20% health and return to Bar with the normal combat action still available.
- Beer and drawer users have separate counter positions. Smoking remains a private choice on the back deck.
- Blackjack has tighter pacing and high-card tie resolution. Room-game openings, action availability and health updates are clearer.

## Easier to learn and play

- A continuous playable walkthrough introduces movement, shooting, healing, roles, theft, escape and spectating; the help library remains available afterwards.
- Solo practice lets you choose **3–7 bots**, discussion/action timings and the shared beer supply.
- Updated title screen, menus, role briefings and results show the whole cast and every winner, including eliminated teammates and personal Swindler wins.
- Combat feedback, weapon motion, voice/spectator behavior, room planning and reconnect handling include the accumulated improvements since 0.2.18.
- Unused assets, obsolete scenes and old generated content have been trimmed from the project.

## Downloads and installation

- **Windows:** extract `LastCall-Windows-0.3.0.zip`, then run `Last Call.exe`. Keep the data folder and DLLs beside it.
- **Mac:** extract `LastCall-macOS-0.3.0.zip`, then open `Last Call.app`. The app supports Intel and Apple Silicon and is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. If macOS requests approval, use its system Open option.
- Verify both archives with `SHA256SUMS-0.3.0.txt`.

This is a private-group playtest release. Automated checks do not replace testing voice, firewalls and social balance with your actual group.
