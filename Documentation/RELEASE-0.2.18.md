# Last Call 0.2.18

Windows x64 and universal macOS playtest release. Use **0.2.18** on every computer in the match.

## Patch notes

- A new title screen uses a scene rendered from the game. Solo practice, LAN setup, and online rooms have clear entry points.
- The lobby, pause menu, and graphics settings share a new layout. Voice, controls, victory rules, and credits have their own pages.
- The Tab card has more space around its steps and shows long private clues one page at a time. Safe, bartender, and room-planning prompts have more room for text.
- The results screen shows every player’s portrait, name, and role. Dead players appear in gray. Every player on the winning team gets a **Winner** badge.
- The Renegade has unlimited bullets. The one-action-per-round rule still applies.
- Shots have clearer recoil, impact effects, and hit feedback. **Reduced motion** lets you turn off the camera kick.
- Spectators can hear the player they watch while their microphone stays in eliminated-player chat.
- The saloon lighting and sunset colors are richer. Practice bots leave the smoking deck available for player plans.

## Installation

Extract the entire archive. On Windows, run `Last Call.exe` with its data folder beside it. On macOS, open `Last Call.app`. The Mac download supports Intel and Apple Silicon. It is ad-hoc signed.

Use `SHA256SUMS-0.2.18.txt` to verify the downloads.
